Project Context & Mandate

This project is a critical intervention under Air Selangor's Emergency and Ad-Hoc Water Improvement Program, initiated in response to directives from state and regulatory authorities. The objective is to secure a resilient, redundant water supply for essential national healthcare infrastructure in the Serdang corridor.

Working with hospital management, Air Selangor diagnosed a persistent low-pressure condition at Hospital Sultan Idris Shah's Cardiology Block. Technical assessment traced the fault to a fundamental design discrepancy introduced during the original planning phase.

Facilities Served
🏥
Hospital Serdang — Hospital Sultan Idris Shah (HSIS)
Malaysia's National Cardiology Referral Centre. Primary off-take point for the new 400mm MSCL main, fitted with bulk meter, electromagnetic flowmeter, and SCADA telemetry.
🏥
Hospital Sultan Idris Shah — Cardiology Block (New)
New cardiology block requiring uninterrupted supply. Chronic low pressure traced to a design discrepancy in the suction tank TWL — project eliminates the pressure deficit at source.
🐾
Hospital Veterinar UPM
Added to scope as a strategic value extension. Route 2 (200mm MSCL/HDPE, 746m) connects from Hospital Serdang, reinforcing the local distribution grid.

Komponen Utama Skop Kerja
  • Pasang 400mm & 200mm ND MSCL (paip lurus dan kepingan pendek) + sambungan & aksesori besi keluli
  • Pasang semua injap (butterfly, sluice, air release, scour) serta bilik injap dan kerja paip washout
  • Pipe jacking berkembar di bawah SKVE (~150m alignment, 300m paip) + single pipe jacking (2 lokasi, 85m)
  • Horizontal Directional Drilling (HDD) — 108m HDPE PN10 250mm di koridor utiliti FT 345
  • Hot tapping — sambungan hidup ke paip sedia 900mm (IOI City Reservoir) dan 200mm (Hospital Veterinar)
  • Pasang electromagnetic flowmeter, RTU panel, dan sistem SCADA telemetri di off-take Hospital Serdang
  • Pasang sistem pengelasan & pagar kawasan flowmeter
  • Kerja elektrikal penuh untuk flowmeter panel, RTU dan sistem bumi
  • Ujian tekanan & kebocoran, sterilisasi/klorinasi, dan commissioning penuh
  • Reinstatement jalan kekal — kaedah mill & pave mengikut spesifikasi JKR/pihak berkuasa tempatan
  • Penyelarasan permit — SPAN, JKR Road Opening, LLM/PLUS (SKVE), KUSEL (koridor utiliti)

Cadangan Jawatankuasa Penilaian Tender (TEC)
Jawatankuasa Penilaian Tender (TEC) ingin mendapatkan sokongan dan cadangan Jawatankuasa Perolehan Pengurusan (MPC) bagi tender 'Proposed Pipe Laying and Improvement Works for Hospital Serdang and Hospital Sultan Idris Shah (Blok Pusat Kardiologi)' (No. Tender: PN0000023850) seperti berikut:
  • 1
    Persetujuan Keputusan Tahap 2 (Teknikal): MPC dimohon untuk meluluskan senarai pembida yang layak secara teknikal dan tidak layak berdasarkan penilaian Tahap 1 dan Tahap 2 yang telah dijalankan oleh TEC.
  • 2
    Kebenaran Membuka Tawaran Komersial: Setelah senarai pembida layak diluluskan, TEC memohon kebenaran MPC untuk membuka tawaran komersial (FOT & BQ) pembida-pembida yang layak secara teknikal sahaja.
  • 3
    Pengesahan Kriteria TACLO: MPC dimohon mengesahkan bahawa pemberian kontrak akan menggunakan kaedah Technically Acceptable & Commercially Lowest Offer (TACLO) dengan penyemakan item-item utama berdasarkan anggaran Air Selangor.
  • 4
    Tempoh Sah Tender: MPC dimaklumkan bahawa tempoh sah tender adalah sehingga 28 Februari 2026. Sebarang kelewatan perlu diuruskan dengan jangkaan penyelarasan tempoh sah.
